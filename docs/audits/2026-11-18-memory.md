# Memory, 2026-11-18 (0.4.9 work)

## Why

dodoradio sent a log after Rustrx closed under their hands while typing
(0.4.8, 136 rooms, background mode without a push distributor). Three
times on 11-17 the app's log simply ends (21:24 while scrolling back,
22:56 after sending, 23:08 while typing) and Rustrx starts again 2 to 12
seconds later: no panic, no error, no shutdown line. That fits a kill
from outside (the low-memory killer) or a crash in native code; the log
cannot tell which. The kernel's lines for those minutes were asked for.
Meanwhile Rustrx on the Jolla Phone 2026 held 470 to 560 MB after half
an hour, so memory was measured.

## Method

On the Jolla Phone 2026 (SFOS 5.2, 11.6 GB, on USB power), one scripted
scenario per build, Rustrx started fresh each time, its memory read from
`/proc/<pid>/smaps` every 5 s (total RSS, anonymous, `[heap]`, the core
library), driven through the dev hook:

1. start, idle 3 min
2. open 25 rooms (6 s each, back)
3. idle 1.5 min
4. open a big room, 40 x `paginate_back` every 2 s
5. back, idle 2 min

glibc's own view came from `malloc_stats()` called through gdb at the
end of a run (arenas, bytes held from the system, bytes in use).

The script is `~/sailfish/work/memtest/mem-run.sh` (author's tree), the
CSVs are in the session's notes. The room picked as "big" differed per
run (the list order changes), so the scroll-back phase compares less
well than the rest.

## Results

End of the scenario:

| Build | RSS | anon | glibc arenas | held / in use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.4.8-7, before | 398 MB | 205 MB | 55 | 242 / 83 MB |
| 0.4.8-8, low-memory store | 359 MB | 171 MB | 25 | 206 / 83 MB |
| 0.4.8-9, and two arenas | 259 MB | 51 MB | 2 | 100 / 76 MB |

By phase, before and after: idle after start 278 MB and 232 MB; opening
25 rooms added 100 MB and 17 MB; scrolling back added almost nothing in
either.

## What it was

- **glibc arenas (M1).** glibc gives a thread that allocates its own
  arena, up to eight per core. tokio's blocking threads come and go for
  every store access, so arenas pile up; each keeps the memory it once
  had. 55 arenas held 242 MB for 83 MB in use. `mallopt(M_ARENA_MAX, 2)`
  at the start of `main()` cuts it (4e2e4cc). `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX` cannot be
  used: sailjail does not pass the environment on. Rustrx under sailjail
  is started fresh by firejail (no booster), so the cap acts before the
  first thread on every start.
- **The SQLite pools (M2).** matrix-sdk-sqlite's default is four
  connections per physical core per store (32 on this phone) with 2 MB
  of page cache each. Its own `with_low_memory_config` (one per core,
  500 KB) is used now, the passphrase as before (75fec28).

## Open

- **M3, the crash cause.** Not the low-memory killer: dodoradio's kernel
  log for 21:24, 22:56 and 23:08 has no kill and no low-memory event
  (reported by the author on 11-18). What is left is a crash in native
  code, which leaves no line in Rustrx's own log; this audit does not
  reach it. A core dump (systemd-coredump) on the next one would.
- **M4, rooms kept.** 17 MB for 25 rooms remains after them; not
  followed further.
- **M5, the timeline after scrolling back.** No growth was seen here, but
  the dev hook's `paginate_back` does not load pictures as scrolling
  does. Dropping loaded history beyond a limit stays an idea until a
  measurement shows the need.

## Also found

- **The stand-in loop (M6).** In dodoradio's log a stand-in started every
  71 s for as long as the app ran (98 times in two hours), each exiting
  with 1 after waiting for the app; its unit restarts on failure.
  Finding the app in front is no failure now (9c22a9d). What started the
  first one 60 s after a handover is not found.
- **A dead end.** Calling `fopen` inside the sandboxed process through
  gdb (for `malloc_info` to a file) ended the process once; `malloc_stats`
  to stderr did not. Do not use the first again.
